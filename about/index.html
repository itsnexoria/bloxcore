About BloxCore

BloxCore is a free community hub for Blox Fruits players: live item values, a trading board, giveaways, crews and guides. Here is how the numbers and the guides are made.

B
Written by The BloxCore Team Last updated November 20, 2026 · First published November 9, 2026 · How we make our guides

How we set values

Values are community estimates, not official prices. They are built from what players are actually trading, how much demand each item has, community votes on whether an item is overpaid, fair or underpaid, and review by the BloxCore team. Because the game changes, values can move after any update.

How often values refresh

The value pages, tier lists and comparisons are regenerated automatically every day from our database, and each page shows when it was last updated. The price history on an item page shows how its value has changed over time.

How we write guides

Guides are written by the BloxCore team. Where a guide states a game fact (level ranges, how raids work), we keep it evergreen and avoid numbers that change often; anything time-sensitive is pulled live from the database instead. Each guide shows the date it was last updated.

Disclosures

BloxCore is supported by advertising and may include affiliate links, which are labelled. We are an independent fan site and are not affiliated with Roblox or the Blox Fruits developers.

Contact and corrections

Spotted something wrong? Tell us in the BloxCore Discord and we will look into it.

FAQ

Is BloxCore affiliated with Roblox or Blox Fruits?

No. BloxCore is an independent fan community and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Roblox Corporation or the developers of Blox Fruits.

How do I report a mistake in a guide or a value?

Tell us in the BloxCore Discord or use the report options on the site. We review reports and correct pages.
