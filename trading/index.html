Stay safe when trading

  • Never share your Roblox password, cookie or 2FA code — nobody legitimate asks.
  • Be wary of brand-new accounts with no vouches, or anyone pushing you to move off-site.
  • Trades for real money are against the rules and a common scam.
  • After a trade, use Mark completed on the listing so both sides get a confirmed trade.
  • Spot a scammer? Use the report button on their profile or listing.
