Best Blox Fruits for the Second Sea (Levels 700–1500)

Enemies in the Second Sea hit harder and some use Haki, so the fruit that carried you through the First Sea may not be enough. Here is what to look for and the picks that hold up.

What changes in the Second Sea

The Second Sea covers levels 700–1500. You unlock it by defeating the Ice Admiral at around level 700. Two things are different from the First Sea:

  • Tougher enemies. Many can ignore the free damage protection elemental fruits gave you before, so survivability matters more than ever.
  • Raids unlock at level 1100. Awakening your fruit becomes a real goal, so pick a fruit you plan to keep. Our raid guide walks through it.

The numbers on each card are BloxCore trading data, not combat stats: value and demand show how a fruit trades, which matters when you buy or sell it. A cheap fruit can still be a great grinder — use the cards for cost and tradability, and the reasoning for how it plays.

Raids and awakening

At level 1100 you can start raids in the Second Sea to earn Fragments and awaken your fruit. Not every fruit awakens, so check before you commit — the raid guide explains the requirements and how much it costs to start.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best grinding fruit in the Second Sea?

Buddha is the most consistently recommended: its tanky form and area damage make long grinding sessions comfortable. Phoenix is a popular alternative because it can heal while you fight.

What levels are the Second Sea?

Levels 700–1500. You unlock it by defeating the Ice Admiral at around level 700.

When can I start raids?

At level 1100 you can start raids in the Second Sea. See the raid guide for what you need.

Do elemental fruits still work in the Second Sea?

They are still playable, but many enemies here can break the protection elemental fruits gave you in the First Sea, so tankier fruits like Buddha or Phoenix are more comfortable.
