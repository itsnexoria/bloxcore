Five questions to ask before you buy

  1. How high is demand? High demand means you can resell it quickly.
  2. What is the trend? Overpaid fruits cost more than usual; underpaid ones may be a good time to buy.
  3. How does it rank? Higher-ranked fruits are bigger purchases — make sure they fit your goal.
  4. Is a permanent version worth the premium? A permanent fruit never expires but costs several times more; check the permanent fruit calculator.
  5. Do you actually need it? For grinding, a cheap fruit is often enough — see the guides for First Sea, Second Sea and Third Sea.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if a Blox Fruit is worth buying?

Look at demand, trend and rank. High demand and a stable or rising trend mean it is easy to resell and likely to hold its price. An "overpaid" trend means you are paying a premium.

What does "overpaid" or "underpaid" mean?

Overpaid means a fruit is trading above what many consider fair value; underpaid means it is trading below. Both are community estimates, so confirm the going rate before you trade.

Is a permanent fruit worth it?

A permanent fruit never expires and is worth several times a physical one. It makes sense if you will keep the fruit for a long time; use the permanent fruit calculator to see the premium for any fruit.

Does a high value mean a fruit is the best to use?

No. Value reflects trading demand, not combat strength. Many players level with cheaper fruits and save expensive ones for trading.
