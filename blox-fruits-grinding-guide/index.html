Blox Fruits Grinding & Leveling Guide

Leveling fast in Blox Fruits comes down to a handful of habits: the right fruit, active experience boosts, the right quests and good server choices. Follow these six steps.

How leveling works

You level by defeating enemies and completing quests. The game is split into three seas with set level ranges: the First Sea (about levels 1–700), the Second Sea (700–1500) and the Third Sea (1500 and up). Quests in each sea are tuned to a level band, so using the quest closest to your level gives the best experience.

Six steps to level faster

  1. Pick a grinding fruitChoose a fruit with area damage and survivability for your sea — see the First, Second and Third Sea guides. Do not overspend: you will swap fruits as you level.
  2. Redeem the latest codesRedeem working codes (including 2x EXP) from the codes page before a long session, and keep an eye on the timer so you use it while grinding.
  3. Take quests in your level rangeAlways grab the quest closest to your level, defeat the whole group and turn it in. Moving up as soon as a higher quest is available keeps experience per kill high.
  4. Kill in groupsPull several enemies together and use area moves. Bosses and boss quests give a large experience bump when you are in range.
  5. Change servers when it is emptyIf enemies are not respawning or other players are farming the same spot, hop to a new server instead of waiting.
  6. Move on at each sea milestoneAt about level 700 unlock the Second Sea, and at 1500 the Third Sea. In the Second Sea, raids unlock at level 1100 for awakening.

Best fruit by sea

The best grinding fruit changes as you move up. Use the guide for the sea you are in:

  • First Sea (levels 1–700) — Light, Magma, Buddha
  • Second Sea (700–1500) — Buddha, Phoenix
  • Third Sea (1500+) — Buddha, Dough, T-Rex

Ready for raids? At level 1100 read the raid guide. Not sure a fruit is worth buying? See the worth-it verdicts.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to level in Blox Fruits?

Use an area-damage fruit, redeem 2x EXP codes, always take the quest closest to your level, and pull enemies in groups. Hop servers when yours is crowded or empty.

What is the best fruit for grinding?

Buddha is the most widely recommended across all seas; Light and Magma are strong early, Phoenix is popular in the Second Sea, and Dragon and Dough show up in the Third Sea. See the sea-by-sea guides for current values.

Where do I find working codes?

We keep a list of working Blox Fruits codes, including experience boosts, on our codes page.

At what level do I unlock the next sea?

You unlock the Second Sea at about level 700 and the Third Sea at level 1500 (after the Second Sea's final quest).
