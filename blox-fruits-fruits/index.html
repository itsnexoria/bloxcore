Frequently asked questions

How many fruits are there in Blox Fruits?

BloxCore currently tracks values for 43 fruits, from Common to Mythical, and the list grows with each update.

Which fruit is worth the most?

West Dragon currently has the highest physical value at 2.76B; the table above is sorted by value and refreshed daily.

What is the difference between physical and permanent value?

A physical fruit is a normal fruit you can trade; a permanent fruit is unlocked for your account and is worth several times more because it cannot be lost or traded away.

Where do the values come from?

They are community estimates built from trading activity, demand and votes, reviewed by the BloxCore team. Read how we set values on our About page.
