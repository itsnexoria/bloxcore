BloxCore brings the whole Blox Fruits grind into one place — trading, bounty hunting, crews, and more, all built around one honest ranking system.

Blox Fruits Trading

Post what you're offering, browse item-for-item trades from the community, and close deals safely with a report system watching your back. See how BloxCore trading works or check current Blox Fruits values.

Bounty Quests

Complete real in-game objectives — boss kills, awakenings, style mastery — submit proof, and earn XP that climbs you from Rookie to Pirate King. Learn about BloxCore bounties.

Crews

Found or join a crew, recruit members through your Roblox username or Discord invite, and climb the crew leaderboard together. Explore Blox Fruits crews.
