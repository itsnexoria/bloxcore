Frequently asked questions

What is BloxCore?

BloxCore is a free community hub for Blox Fruits players: trading listings, PvP and sea event matchmaking, daily bounties, giveaways, combo guides, crews and leaderboards, all in one place.

Is BloxCore free?

Yes. Everything on the site is free to use. Create an account to post listings, complete bounties, join crews and enter giveaways.

How do I sign up?

Use the Sign In button and continue with Roblox or Discord. Your BloxCore username is created from your account name.

Is trading on BloxCore safe?

BloxCore doesn't verify or guarantee any trade. Listings are just a way to find other players, so trade at your own risk and double-check what you're being offered. Trade listings auto-delete after 24 hours.

Where do item values come from?

Values shown on the trading page are community-estimated, not guaranteed. Use them as a guide alongside the Fair Trade badge and the value history for each item.

How do bounties and XP work?

Complete bounties on the Bounty Board to earn XP and level up. Daily, weekly and monthly bounties reset on a schedule, and standing bounties are always available. A daily login streak also earns bonus XP.

How do PvP and Sea Event listings work?

Post a match or a sea event server, or join someone else's. These listings auto-expire after 1 hour so the board stays fresh.

How do giveaways work?

Enter any active giveaway for a chance to win in-game prizes. It's one entry per giveaway, and winners are shown under Past Winners once a giveaway ends.

Can I message other players?

You can message your friends. Messages older than 7 days are automatically deleted, but the conversation itself stays.
