Blox Fruits Giveaways: How to Enter (and Host) Safely

Giveaways are one of the easiest ways to land a rare fruit for free — and one of the most scammed corners of the Blox Fruits community. Here's how to tell the difference.

Why Giveaways Are Everywhere in Blox Fruits

Because so much of the game's economy runs on traded items rather than cash, giving away a fruit costs the host nothing but the item itself — no payment processor, no real money changing hands. That low barrier is exactly why giveaways are one of the most common ways content creators, crews, and community members build goodwill and grow an audience. It's also exactly why they're an easy target for scammers running a fake giveaway purely to farm engagement with no intention of ever delivering a prize.

Common Giveaway Scam Patterns

A few patterns show up constantly in fake giveaways, and knowing them is most of the protection you need:

  • Pay-to-enter giveaways — a legitimate giveaway never asks you to send an item, Robux, or money to "confirm" your entry. That's not a giveaway, that's the scam.
  • No verifiable host history — a brand-new account promising an expensive prize with zero track record of ever delivering one before.
  • Winners that are never announced or contactable — if a giveaway "ends" and there's no public winner announcement, or the winner can never actually be reached, that's a strong sign it was never real.
  • Urgency pressure — "only 2 minutes left, DM me now" framing designed to make you act before you think it through.

What a Legitimate Giveaway Looks Like

A real giveaway is free to enter, has a clear and public end time, and the host has some track record — either past giveaways they've actually delivered, or a reputation within a community that would notice if they didn't. The best sign of all is proof after the fact: a host who shows the actual trade or delivery to the winner once it's done, not just a screenshot of an entry list.

How BloxCore Giveaways Work

Every giveaway submitted on BloxCore goes through staff review before it ever goes live on the Giveaways page — nothing gets posted automatically. Once a giveaway is approved and running, any signed-in player can enter, and when it ends, a winner is selected. To close the loop, the host is expected to actually deliver the prize and can mark the giveaway completed by uploading a screenshot proving the delivery happened — and that completion proof goes through a second round of staff review before it's marked verified in the giveaway's public history. That history stays visible on the Giveaways page long after the giveaway ends, so anyone can check whether a host has actually followed through before.

Why the Review Step Exists

Fake or troll giveaways — ones posted purely as engagement bait with no real intention of delivering a prize — are a direct violation of BloxCore's rules and can get an account banned from the site. That's a deliberately serious consequence, because a fake giveaway doesn't just waste the time of everyone who entered — it erodes trust in every other giveaway on the board. The review process, both when a giveaway is submitted and again when it's marked completed, exists specifically to keep that trust intact.

Hosting Your Own

If you're planning to give away a fruit or limited you've earned or picked up, submitting it through BloxCore gets it in front of the whole community rather than just your own followers, and the review + proof-of-delivery system means entrants can trust it's real. Just be ready to actually deliver — that commitment is the whole point.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I tell if a Blox Fruits giveaway is fake?

Watch for red flags: being asked to pay or send an item to enter, a host with no track record, no clear public winner announcement, or heavy pressure to act immediately. A real giveaway is free to enter and has a transparent end time.

Does BloxCore verify giveaways before they go live?

Yes — every submitted giveaway goes through staff review before it's posted, and marking a giveaway completed requires the host to upload proof of delivery, which goes through a second review.

What happens if someone hosts a fake giveaway on BloxCore?

Troll or fake giveaways — never intending to deliver the prize — are a violation of BloxCore's rules and can get the host's account banned from the site.

Can I see a host's past giveaway history?

Yes — completed and verified giveaways stay visible in the public giveaway history, so you can check whether a host has actually delivered before entering theirs.
