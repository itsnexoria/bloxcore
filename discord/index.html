How the Discord and BloxCore work together

  1. Join the serverOpen the invite, accept it, and choose your roles in self-roles.
  2. Sign in to BloxCore with DiscordOne tap — no new password, and your Discord profile picture becomes your BloxCore avatar.
  3. Use the toolsCheck item values, post on the trading board, enter reviewed giveaways and add items to your watchlist to get an alert when someone offers them.

Trade safely

A few habits that avoid most scams: check the current value of what you're trading, keep the whole conversation in public channels, never send first to someone with no history, and screenshot everything. Not sure about an offer? Run it through the trade calculator, or read the trading guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the BloxCore Discord server free to join?

Yes. The server is free — open the invite link, accept it, and pick your roles in the self-roles channel. You only need a Discord account.

What can I do in the Blox Fruits Discord?

Trade fruits and limiteds in the trading channel, enter giveaways (and see the proof when they finish), find players for sea events and trials, join or advertise a crew, ask for help, and jump into voice chat with the community.

Do I need a Discord account to use BloxCore?

No. You can browse values, tier lists and the trading board without an account. Signing in with Discord is the fastest way to join the site (one tap, and your Discord profile picture is used), but email sign-in works too.

How do I trade safely in the Discord?

Check an item's current value on BloxCore before you agree to anything, keep the whole trade in public channels, never send items first to someone with no history, and report anyone who pressures you. Staff handle reports and tickets.

How do I host a giveaway?

Submit it on BloxCore's giveaways page. Every giveaway is reviewed by staff before it goes live, and the host uploads proof of delivery when it ends.

How do I report a scammer?

Open a ticket in the Discord server or use the report button on BloxCore. Include screenshots and the user's name so staff can review it.
