Blox Fruits Raid Guide: How to Start Raids and Awaken Fruits

Raids are how you earn Fragments to awaken your fruit. Here is what you need, where to go and what to expect, in six steps.

What you need

  • Level 1100 and access to the Second Sea.
  • A fruit that can be awakened. Not every fruit can; check that yours is before you spend anything. Our worth-it guide shows how each fruit trades if you are choosing one to raid with.
  • A Special Microchip from the Mysterious Scientist, and ideally a few teammates.

How to start a raid

  1. Reach level 1100Raids start at level 1100, in the Second Sea. Unlock the Second Sea first by defeating the Ice Admiral at around level 700.
  2. Go to the raid labTravel to the raid laboratory in the Second Sea (the Hot and Cold area) and find the Mysterious Scientist.
  3. Buy a Special MicrochipBuy a Special Microchip from the Mysterious Scientist. At the time of writing it costs 100,000 Beli or a physical fruit.
  4. Equip the fruit you want to awakenHold the fruit you are raiding with, then use the microchip on the raid console to pick the raid type and start it.
  5. Clear all five wavesDefeat the enemies on each island, move up the islands and finish the final wave. Bring friends — raids are much easier as a team.
  6. Collect Fragments and awakenFinishing a raid awards Fragments. Spend them with the Mysterious Entity to awaken your fruit, which unlocks stronger versions of its moves.

Awakening explained

Awakening upgrades the moves of a fruit that supports it. You earn Fragments from raids and spend them with the Mysterious Entity; once awakened, you can unlock the upgraded versions of each move. Plan on several raids, because the Fragment cost adds up.

  • Check that your fruit can be awakened before you start.
  • Bring a team — it speeds up clears and makes the later waves safer.
  • Grinding levels first helps: see the grinding guide.

Frequently asked questions

What level do I need to start raids?

Level 1100, in the Second Sea.

Where do I buy the raid microchip?

From the Mysterious Scientist in the Second Sea raid lab. At the time of writing it costs 100,000 Beli or a physical fruit; prices can change with updates.

How many waves are in a raid?

Five waves, one per island.

How do I awaken my fruit?

Earn Fragments by finishing raids, then speak to the Mysterious Entity to awaken the fruit you are holding. Awakened fruits unlock stronger versions of their moves.

Can I do raids alone?

Yes, but they are much easier with teammates, especially later raids. You can find players in our Discord or on the services board.
