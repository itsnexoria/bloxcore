Blox Fruits Crews: Build One, Join One, or Go to War

A good crew turns solo grinding into a team effort. Here's how crews work in Blox Fruits, and how BloxCore's crew system adds a leaderboard and crew-vs-crew wars on top.

Why Crews Matter in Blox Fruits

Blox Fruits is playable entirely solo, but a crew changes the experience — you've got people to run raids with, trade within a trusted group, and get help finding fruit spawns or clearing a boss you've been stuck on. Crews also carry a social identity: your crew tag shows up next to your name, and a well-known crew can carry real reputation on its own, separate from any individual member's stats.

Founding vs. Joining

Starting your own crew means you set the identity — the name, the tag, the recruiting standards — but it also means doing the work of actually recruiting and keeping members engaged. Joining an existing crew is faster and gives you an instant group to play with, but you're working within rules and a culture someone else already set. Neither is strictly better; it depends on whether you want to build something or just want people to play with right now.

How BloxCore Crews Work

On BloxCore, any player can submit a crew with a name, tag, description, and logo. Other players join by verifying through the crew leader's Roblox username or a Discord server invite the leader provides, which keeps membership tied to something real rather than just a self-reported list. Once you're in a crew, your profile shows the crew tag, and the crew itself gets its own page listing all current members.

The Crew Leaderboard

Every crew on BloxCore is ranked on a dedicated crew leaderboard, separate from the individual player leaderboard, based on the combined XP and activity of its members. This gives smaller, tightly-knit crews a real way to compete with larger ones — a crew of a dozen highly active members can outrank a crew of fifty mostly-inactive ones. Top crews each month are awarded a title that shows on every member's profile for as long as the crew holds that spot.

Crew Wars

Crews on BloxCore can issue a formal war call to another crew, and once accepted, members from both sides face off — the outcome is tracked and feeds into a separate crew wars leaderboard ranked purely by win/loss record. Because outcomes affect a public leaderboard, results are staff-confirmed rather than self-declared by either crew's leader, and a war with no verifiable evidence of the outcome is recorded as a tie rather than a win for either side. This keeps the wars leaderboard meaningful instead of turning into whichever crew leader is more willing to claim a win.

What Makes a Crew Actually Good

The strongest crews aren't necessarily the biggest ones — they're the ones with an actual identity: a clear focus (PvP-heavy, trading-focused, casual grinding, raid clearing), active leadership, and members who are actually online and playing together instead of a long member list that's mostly names. If you're picking a crew to join, look at recent activity and how the crew ranks on the leaderboard relative to its member count, not just the raw number of members.

Starting Your Own

If you'd rather build something from scratch, the Crews page is where you submit a new crew — pick a name and tag that aren't already taken, write a description that tells potential members what your crew is actually about, and set your verification method so people know how to prove they've joined. From there it's on you to recruit, and the crew leaderboard will reflect exactly how that effort pays off.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a Blox Fruits crew on BloxCore?

Find a crew on the Crews page and verify your membership through the leader's Roblox username or the Discord invite they've provided — this keeps membership tied to something real rather than a self-reported list.

What's the difference between the crew leaderboard and crew wars?

The crew leaderboard ranks crews by combined member XP and activity. Crew wars is a completely separate leaderboard based purely on win/loss record from formal crew-vs-crew war calls.

Can a crew leader declare their own war win?

No — outcomes are staff-confirmed to keep the wars leaderboard meaningful. If there's no verifiable evidence of the result, the war is recorded as a tie for both sides.

Is a bigger crew always better?

Not necessarily — the crew leaderboard rewards active member XP, so a smaller, highly active crew can outrank a much larger but mostly inactive one.
