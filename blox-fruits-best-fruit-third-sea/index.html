What changes in the Third Sea

The Third Sea opens at level 1500, after you finish the Second Sea's final quest. Enemies come in large groups and bosses are tougher, so area damage and a way to survive hits matter more than raw single-target damage.

The numbers on each card are BloxCore trading data, not combat stats: value and demand show how a fruit trades, which matters when you buy or sell it. A cheap fruit can still be a great grinder — use the cards for cost and tradability, and the reasoning for how it plays.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best fruit for the Third Sea?

It depends on whether you grind, fight bosses or PvP. Buddha, Dough, Dragon, T-Rex and Kitsune are the names that come up most; the picks above show how each one trades today so you can balance power against price.

What level is the Third Sea?

It opens at level 1500, once you have completed the Second Sea's final quest.

Does the best fruit change with updates?

Yes. Updates add and rebalance fruits, which also moves their trading value. The picks here lean on fruits that have stayed useful, and the numbers on each card are refreshed daily.

Is it better to buy or trade for a Third Sea fruit?

Trading is often cheaper. Check the live value, compare listings on the trading board and run the offer through the calculator.
