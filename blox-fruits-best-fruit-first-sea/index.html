The First Sea is where you learn the game, so the best fruit is one that is cheap to use, safe to level with and easy to sell later. Here is how to choose, plus our top picks with live trading data.

What matters in the First Sea

The First Sea covers levels 1–700. Enemies are slow and weak, so the fruit that works best is the one that lets you kill groups quickly without taking damage. Three things decide that:

  • Area damage. Moves that hit several enemies at once shorten every quest.
  • Survivability. Elemental fruits such as Flame, Ice, Magma and Light can let you shrug off the basic attacks of enemies below your level, which makes early grinding far less painful.
  • Cost and resale. You will swap fruits a lot early on, so a fruit that holds some trade value is better than one nobody wants.

The numbers on each card are BloxCore trading data, not combat stats: value and demand show how a fruit trades, which matters when you buy or sell it. A cheap fruit can still be a great grinder — use the cards for cost and tradability, and the reasoning for how it plays.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best fruit for the First Sea?

There is no single answer, but Light, Magma and Buddha are the names that come up most. Light and Magma are strong and comparatively affordable; Buddha is the best all-rounder if you can pay for it, because it stays useful in every sea.

What levels are the First Sea?

The First Sea covers roughly levels 1 to 700. At about level 700 you defeat the Ice Admiral to unlock the Second Sea.

Is it worth buying a Mythical fruit this early?

Usually not just for grinding. Mythical fruits cost a lot and the First Sea does not need one — a cheaper elemental or Buddha levels you almost as fast. If you do own one, check its trading value before deciding to keep it.

Should I trade for my first fruit?

It can be cheaper than buying it. Check the current value on BloxCore, compare a few offers, and use the trade calculator so you do not overpay.
